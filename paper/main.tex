\begin{abstract}
Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) can substantially improve
language-model performance on the tasks used for post-training, but its gains
do not transfer uniformly to unseen benchmarks.  We study the prospective,
directed transfer problem: given a source dataset and an unlabeled target task,
can we predict whether RLVR will transfer before training, and can that signal
be used to select better source data?  We introduce \method, a framework based
on Reasoning Transition Graphs, Directed Reasoning Coverage, and target-label-
free data selection.  We further introduce a capability-flow analysis that
separates empirical support expansion from probability sharpening and
