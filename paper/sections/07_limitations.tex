\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our framework depends on an automatically extracted reasoning representation,
whose taxonomy may omit relevant operations and whose errors may vary across
domains.  The label-free target-headroom estimate uses answer disagreement and
may underestimate confidently incorrect behavior.  Finite-sample capability
flow establishes empirical support expansion, not proof that the base policy
assigned exactly zero probability to a correct solution.  Our claims are also
limited by the evaluated model scales, RLVR optimizers, verifiable task
families, and available compute.  Finally, deduplication and temporal controls
can reduce but cannot completely rule out contamination from opaque
pretraining corpora.  We report these factors explicitly and restrict each
claim to the settings covered by the confirmatory experiments.
